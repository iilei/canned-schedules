# Nightly Batch

Quiet during the day, heavy processing at night from Monday to Saturday, and switched off on Sunday.

> [!SCHEDULE:META] Quiet days and heavy processing at night

> [!SCHEDULE:BASE] Minimum

> [!SCHEDULE:TIER] Maximum
> - `01:00` to `05:00` MON-SAT

> [!SCHEDULE:TIER] Off
> - `00:00` to `00:00` SUN
