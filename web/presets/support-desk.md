# Support Desk

Which channels the support desk answers, per hour of the week. The states aren't capacity tiers here: the preset
replaces them with the desk's own (see Equivalent CLI flags below the editor), and the built-in colours go round
robin over them. Phone hours sit on top of the chat hours, because later ranges win where they overlap.

> [!SCHEDULE-META] Channels customers can reach
> - x-team: `customer-care`

> [!SCHEDULE-BASE] Closed

> [!SCHEDULE-STATE] Email
> - `07:00` to `21:00` MON-FRI
> - `09:00` to `17:00` SAT

> [!SCHEDULE-STATE] Chat
> - `08:00` to `20:00` MON-FRI
> - `10:00` to `14:00` SAT

> [!SCHEDULE-STATE] Phone
> - `09:00` to `12:00` MON-FRI
> - `13:00` to `17:00` MON-THU
> - `13:00` to `15:00` FRI
