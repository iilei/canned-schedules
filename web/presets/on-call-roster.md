# On-call Roster

One week of a follow-the-sun on-call rota for a roster of 30 people (the names are made up). Every person is a state,
so the preset sets the states, their colours and their letters for the ASCII preview as config overrides: see
Equivalent CLI flags below the editor. Weekdays have four 6-hour shifts, the weekend five shorter ones.

> [!SCHEDULE-META] Follow-the-sun rota for a roster of 30
> - x-team: `sre-guild`
> - x-escalation: `#oncall-sre`
